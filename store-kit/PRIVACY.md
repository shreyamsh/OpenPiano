# Privacy Policy for Open Piano

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

Open Piano is a free, open-source piano app for Windows.

**Data collection.** Open Piano does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features.

**Audio.** The app generates sound locally on your device and plays it through your chosen audio output. It does not record audio and does not use your microphone.

**Third parties.** The app does not send data to any third party. Microsoft may collect standard app acquisition and crash data through the Microsoft Store, which is governed by Microsoft's own privacy statement.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.

**Contact.** Questions can be opened as an issue at https://github.com/shreyamsh/OpenPiano/issues
